Four directions for elder & guardian

Each variant restyles the two flagship screens — the elder home and the guardian dashboard — while keeping the existing layout and information intact. They differ in palette and in styling philosophy, so you can judge feel, not features. Pick one and we'll decide whether to implement it.

Layout unchanged from current build 393px phone canvas Same SOS · elder-switcher · quick-actions · alerts
01
Clinical Teal · crisp & flat

Ocean Trust

A cool, medical-grade palette built on teal and cyan. Tighter corner radii, hairline card borders and restrained shadows give it a clean, clinical, "this is a health device" credibility. Reads as calm and dependable rather than playful.

teal · cyan · tint · indigo · cool grey

Dark Navy + Mint · night-friendly

Midnight Care

A dark, OLED-friendly world where a mint-emerald accent glows against deep navy. Easy on aging eyes at night, low battery draw, and it makes the red SOS unmistakable. The most modern and "premium device" of the set — a real departure from the current light theme.
